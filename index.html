1 Hitung Eksponen Tinggi Tekan H^(3/2)
Rumus: H^(3/2) = H × √H
H^(3/2) = 4.0 × √4.0 = 4.0 × 2.00 = 8.00
H^(3/2) = 8.00 m^(3/2)
2 Debit Pelimpah (Q) & Tonase Air / Detik
Q = C × L × H^(3/2)
Q = 2.20 × 60 × 8.00 = 1,056 m³/s
Massa = 1,056 m³/s × 1 Ton/m³
Q = 1,056 m³/s ≈ 1,056 Ton/s (63,360 m³/menit • 3,801,600 m³/jam)

t_pool = V_olimpiade / Q
